D.2 Clipping Conditional Quantile Interval Length
To mitigate dictator effect, we have proposed to clip large matched conditional
quantile interval lengths with an upper bound, which is set to η
100 . More con-
cretely, we set η = 10. To demonstrate the efficacy of this strategy on various
datasets, we compute the performance metrics of QCAD by varying the hyper-
parameter η ∈ {0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, ...,11, 12, 15, 20, 30, 40, N one}, where None
means no clipping is performed.
